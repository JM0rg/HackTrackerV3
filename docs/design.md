# Design

Player-scorer slowpitch. Our lineup only. Opponent = name + runs. No account required to score.

**You first.** Every install has a local Person. App opens on You. Team is optional (Team tab empty until they create one). More = appearance + optional sign-in.

**Games.** Team: full lineup; the engine advances runners and the scorer corrects runs with a chip under the play. Personal: just you, in one of two scopes chosen on Log my game. *My At-Bats Only* keeps plate appearances and nothing else. *My At-Bats and Team Scores* also keeps both teams' scores: you tap + RUN for teammates, END HALF when our half ends by hand, and the opponent's half is the same card a team game uses; home or away decides who bats first and a line score falls out. Opponent and playing-with are optional. Playing-with has a Pick team button: choose a saved personal team to fill the name and tag the game, add a new one from the same sheet, or just type a name and save nothing. Personal teams are names only, per person, and separate from club Teams.

**The log.** A game is plate appearances plus finished opponent halves in one sequence. Score, outs, inning, bases and the batting order are all replayed from it, so a correction anywhere replays everything after it. Undo pops the newest entry of either kind; undoing an opponent half reopens its tally rather than dropping the runs.

**Field Mode.** One card. The score is the hero: two big numerals with US and THEM under them, then one situation pill (half, inning, outs on a team; the opponent in a personal game). Under that, the batter at 27pt with "2 for 3", hit types as small tags and RBI as a word; a personal game drops the name and shows YOUR DAY instead. The last play sits right above the diamond, in words ("Mike singled"), with its undo. The only scoring control is the batter's chip on home plate. Drag it to a base (1B/2B/3B), past the fence (HR), flick it down (OUT), The row under the diamond then asks what the drag left open, and nothing is filed until it is answered: an out asks Fly · Ground · Line · K, first base asks 1B · BB · ROE · FC, a personal hit asks RBI. A team double, triple or home run files on release. The chip is locked while a question stands, and the row carries a cancel; a run on a fly or line out is a sac fly, on a grounder an RBI groundout. Runners are chips that run when a play files; tap one to send them home. Swipe the name down to undo, sideways to move through the order. Tap the last-play line to fix it in words (error, forced runner, struck out, run scored) or change it outright. Tap the score line for the log. Their half is a full card: tap for a run, swipe up to end it. Dark scoreboard in both themes.

**Career.** You stats by `personId`. Filters (only if they play on a team): All, Free agent, per team.
